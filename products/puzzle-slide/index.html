Help

Common questions

How do I play?

Tap a piece next to the empty space to slide it in. Arrange the pieces to match the target picture. In every target the empty space is in the top-right corner. Every puzzle is scrambled with real moves, so it can always be solved.

What game modes are there?

Two colors (7 red and 8 blue), Four colors (red, blue, yellow and green), and Pairs, where each character has to sit next to its object: Rabbit & Carrot, Horse & Hay ball, Child & Playground and Family & Home. Choose from one to four pairs.

How many puzzles can I play for free?

Three puzzles a day. A puzzle counts when you make its first move, and reshuffling the same puzzle does not count again. The one-time full unlock removes the limit. There is no subscription and there are no ads.

I bought the full game. How do I restore it on a new device?
  1. Sign in to the device with the same Apple ID or Google account you used to buy it.
  2. Open Puzzle Slide and tap Unlock everything on the home screen.
  3. Choose Restore purchase and answer the grown-up question.

Your purchase is restored for free. Family Sharing works on Apple devices if the purchase is shared with your family.

Why is there a question before buying?

Buying and restoring sit behind a parental gate, so children cannot make a purchase by accident.

How do I get a refund?

Payments are handled by Apple or Google, so refunds are requested from them. On Apple devices, use reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, follow Google’s steps at support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637.

How do I use my own pictures or change the colors?

From the home screen, open Customize colors, pictures & theme. Pick the four piece colors, replace any pair picture with a photo from your library, or choose an Automatic, Light or Dark theme. While playing, the Customize button opens the settings for that game type.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. A photo you choose is shrunk to a small square and stored only inside the app on your device. Removing it in the app, or deleting the app, removes it. See the privacy policy.

How do I see moves, time or the “to fix” counter?

They are hidden by default so the game stays simple. Turn them on in Customize.
