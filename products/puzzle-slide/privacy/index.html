Puzzle Slide

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Puzzle Slide is a sliding-puzzle game for children and families. We built it to collect nothing about you or your child.

What we collect

Nothing. Puzzle Slide has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs that collect data. The app does not send any information to us or to anyone else.

Photos

If you choose to put your own pictures on the puzzle pieces, the app asks for access to your photo library. The picture you select is shrunk to a small square and saved only on your device, inside the app. It is never uploaded or shared. Removing the picture in the app, or deleting the app, removes it.

Settings and progress

Your color, picture and theme choices, the number of free puzzles played today, and whether you bought the full game are saved only on your device.

Purchases

The optional one-time “full unlock” purchase is handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your name, payment details or account. Purchases sit behind a parental gate so children cannot buy by accident. See Apple’s privacy policy and Google’s privacy policy for how they handle purchases.

Children

Puzzle Slide is designed for children. Because we collect no personal information, we comply with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the GDPR and similar laws by not collecting data at all.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Questions? Email us at support@tmmw.app.
