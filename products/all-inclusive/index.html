FAQ

Questions

What does All Inclusive include?

Every TMMW plugin on every host we support: Clips Exporter, Text Replacer and Selector for Premiere Pro. Each one is the full version, with nothing held back for a higher tier.

How does the free trial work?

New subscribers get 14 days free. Your first payment is taken when the trial ends, and you can cancel before then from your account page. The trial is available once per account.

Can I switch between monthly and yearly?

Yes. Open your account page and choose Manage subscription to change plan, change the number of seats, update your card, download invoices or cancel.

What happens if I cancel?

Your subscription runs until the end of the period you’ve paid for, and access ends then. You can subscribe again at any time.

Which versions of Premiere Pro are supported?

Clips Exporter and Selector support Premiere Pro 2020 to 2027, and Text Replacer supports Premiere Pro 2021 to 2027.

I bought a plugin on aescripts. Do I need this?

No. Licenses bought on aescripts keep working and are managed there. All Inclusive is for anyone who wants every plugin under one subscription.
